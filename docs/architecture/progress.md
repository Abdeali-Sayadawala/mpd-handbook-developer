## Rules that are enforced by tests today
- Another customer's property, document, KYC record or registration is **404**, never 403.
- OTPs and invite and share tokens are stored hashed; passwords are argon2id.
- Only a masked identity number is stored; a full Aadhaar number is refused.
- Uploads are checked by file contents, 20 MB limit, de-duplicated, locked while another record relies on them.
- One active owner per property (also enforced by the database).
- A price is only ever computed on the server; extra price fields in a request are rejected.
- Whole paise only; half-up rounding; draft and retired price lists never used.
- **Purchaser control:** either the property owner or the project owner may buy; only the purchaser can change what they bought, in both directions; the other party sees it exists but not its price; strangers get 404; ops can cancel with a recorded reason. A change of owner does not take over earlier purchases.
- **Plans** are made by admins, never fixed in code, and **have no typed-in price**: the price is calculated per property from each service's own price x how many times it is done, less service and plan discounts (rounded half up to the paisa). A plan of only lump-sum services is fixed. The order keeps the whole breakdown; a subscription keeps its visits, starts when its order is paid, ends after its last day, and is cancelled with its unpaid order. Same purchaser rule as orders.
- **Property history** (`GET /v1/properties/:id/history`) belongs to the property, not the owner: orders, plans and ownership changes stay when the owner changes. A new owner sees them without the previous owner's name or prices. Finished jobs, their checklists and photos are part of it too (built 1-Dec-2026): the jobs module lists them and a new owner opens the photos through a logged link even though the files sit in the vault of whoever ordered the job.
- **Cases with no area (M7.3, M7.5):** a request with no property has no pincode, so its case is in nobody's pool; only operations see it (not a franchise or master franchise) and give it to a live provider that offers the service; operations are alerted when it opens and again after 24 hours; only the provider earns (the franchise and master franchise shares are not paid, MPD keeps them); doing the work, delivery and rating are the same. Full table: `legal-services-plan.md`, "Cases with an area and cases with no area".
- **Earnings for legal cases (M7.5):** nothing is earned until the case is COMPLETED (in progress and cancelled cases earn nothing); the base is the accepted offer's professional fee and other charges less the discount (government fees and stamp duty excluded); each level at most once however often the sweep runs; each organisation sees only its own share; a case with no area pays only the provider; the database refuses an earning without exactly one source.
- **Doing a legal case (M7.4):** nothing can be ticked or delivered before work has begun; steps go in order and only the provider that holds the case can tick, deliver or complete (another provider gets 404, operations, franchise and customer 403); the same file twice is refused; a delivered document is in the customer's own vault, folder Legal cases, and the customer cannot delete it; completing needs every step and one document; a completed case cannot be changed, cancelled or re-completed; the customer rates a completed case once (1 to 5; other customers get 404, the provider 403); the database refuses a COMPLETED case without a time and a rating on an unfinished case; the chat stays open after completion.
- **Legal cases (M7.3):** a case exists only for a request whose order is PAID, once; only a provider that is live with staff, holds the pincode, offers the service and has not turned the case down sees it in its pool; the customer is hidden until accepted and the request, documents and messages are closed to a pool viewer; two providers accepting at once: exactly one wins; another provider, another franchise and other customers get 404; a provider cannot give a case back after work began; a case with no area appears in no pool and only operations can give it to a provider that offers the service; the upline can pick only an eligible provider and only while the case is open; only operations cancel; messages open once a provider has the case, close when cancelled, customers see first names, the franchise can read but not write; the SLA alert is sent once; the provider never sees the offer or the price discussion.
- **Review and offer (M7.2):** only operations review, and only a request that was sent (a draft is 404); asking for more makes the request editable again and withdraws an open offer; the total is worked out by the server from the lines and the discount (a client-sent total is refused); an offer needs a professional fee, no duplicate lines, at most 12 lines, a discount only with a reason and less than the lines, 1 to 30 days; one open offer at a time (database rule); accepting makes exactly one order for the exact total with one line per fee and the discount on the order, the order may have no property, accepting twice, after the last day, by another customer or by staff is refused; withdrawing is possible until an offer is accepted (then the order is cancelled under Orders); cancelling the order closes the request; a refused or declined request cannot be reviewed again; the customer sees the reason for a refusal.
- **Legal requests (M7.1):** a request keeps the form version it started with, so a later edit of the form never changes it; a customer sees only their own requests and starts one only for an active professional service that has a form (anything else, and someone else's property, is 404); answers are checked against the type of their question (text length, a number, a real date, yes or no, one of the options) and a refused save changes nothing, even when part of it was fine; a document goes only to an item of the checklist, no more files than the item allows, no unknown item, no duplicate file; submitting needs every required answer and document and says what is missing; after submitting, answers and documents cannot change and files stay locked in the vault; withdrawing a draft or a submitted request is possible once, and unlocks the files; drafts are invisible to operations; staff and signed-out callers are refused on the customer addresses; at most 20 open drafts; the database ties the status to the submitted and withdrawn times.
- **Legal service forms (M7.0):** only operations and the super admin write forms; only services of kind PROFESSIONAL have one (a field service is 404); every save is a new version and earlier versions stay exactly as saved; a refused save changes nothing; a choice needs at least two different options and options on any other type are refused; the same question twice in one section, the same document twice or two progress steps with the same name are refused; at most 60 questions, 30 documents, 12 steps; a form with any content needs 2 to 12 progress steps; an empty form is allowed (no form yet); unknown fields are refused; a customer reads only the latest form of an active professional service (inactive, field and unknown services are 404).
- **Earnings (M6.5):** the levels together can never take more than 100% of the job price (checked for the defaults and for every category); only a CLOSED job earns, once per level (database rule); a cancelled job and a job with no provider earn nothing; the share is rounded down so the parts never exceed the whole; the percentage and base are copied into the earning, so changing a rule never changes what was earned; the base includes only extra work the customer approved and paid; the franchise and master franchise are those whose territories hold the job's pincode; a payout is recorded by one admin and approved or refused by a different one (service check and a database rule); a bank reference cannot back two live payouts; recording covers only ACCRUED earnings of that organisation and refuses anyone else's or ones already in a payout; refusing or withdrawing releases the earnings; a partner sees only its own organisations (another organisation's id is 404); workers and customers are refused.
- **Reports (M6.4):** the numbers are scoped by role on the server (operations all; a franchise only the pincodes of its territories, so another area's franchise sees nothing of ours; a provider admin only its organisation; a worker only their own jobs; customers are refused); money is shown only to operations and franchises; a period is at most 366 India-time days and a bad date is refused; a job counts in the period it was created; "completed" means work done, checked or closed; "missed the limit" means accepted after the limit or still waiting in the pool past it; the spreadsheet is scoped the same way, audited, limited to 5000 rows, not for workers, and never lets a cell run as a formula.
- **Chat (M6.3):** only people on the job can read or write (anyone else gets 404); a franchise or master franchise in the area can read but not write; chat is closed until a provider has taken the job and closed for writing once the job is closed or cancelled (reading stays open); text is 1 to 1000 characters after trimming (empty is refused); the customer sees first names only; the writer is never notified of their own message; a busy chat sends one notification at a time per person until they open it; unread counts are per person; writing counts as having read what came before.
- **Reminders (M6.2):** every reminder is sent once (dedupe key per person and event, so running the sweep again changes nothing); a job accepted in time is never alerted; a visit far away or paid orders and ended plans are not reminded; a moved visit is reminded again; the customer's reminder shows their own time zone; one area failing does not stop the others.
- **Notifications (M6.1):** a person reads and marks read only their own notifications (nobody else's, even by id); the same event never notifies the same person twice (database rule); a notification that fails never undoes the business change; email is skipped when the person switched it off, but the in-app notification is always made; an email row is retried at growing gaps and marked FAILED after five tries; two workers never send the same row (guarded claim); people who are not active users are skipped.
- **Extra work (M5.2c):** only the assigned worker raises a request, only while the work is in progress, with at least one photo, and only one request is open per job (database rule); totals are computed on the server in whole paise; a request over 50% of the job's price (or with no known price) needs a provider admin or ops sign-off before the customer sees it; the customer never sees a draft or an unsigned request; only the customer who bought the job decides; the job stays `AWAITING_EXTRA_PAYMENT` until the extra order is PAID by an approved maker-checker payment, then continues; declined, withdrawn, cancelled (by the provider, ops or by cancelling the order) mean the work goes on as first agreed; an order line may have neither service nor plan only for extra work (database rule).
- **Checklists and photos (M5.2b):** a new checklist version never changes a job that has started (it keeps its copy); only the assigned worker answers or adds photos, only while the work is in progress, and an answer must fit its step type; a job cannot be finished with a required step, step photo or before/after photo missing; the same photo cannot be added twice; photos are locked in the customer's vault; place needs both latitude and longitude (database rule); the customer sees the report only after the work is done and only for their own job.
- **Provider areas:** a provider's own admin can create, change and delete its territories; the pincodes must lie inside the area of its original franchise or of a franchise that accepted it (so every pincode it serves is under a franchise it is registered in); another provider cannot touch them; a franchise still cannot change its own area.
- **Doing the work (M5.2a):** a code works once, for 10 minutes, for five tries, and only the customer who bought the job can get it; only the assigned worker starts or finishes; a started job cannot be cancelled, released or reassigned away; only the provider or operations check work and a checked job can be rated once (1 to 5; the database refuses a rating before the check); overriding a code needs operations and a reason; the end of an unrecorded site visit cannot be overridden; a worker's bad property corrections change nothing.
- **Jobs (M5.1):** exactly one provider can accept a job (guarded update, tested with two at once); a provider that is not eligible (area, service, suspended, inactive or under an inactive franchise) cannot see or take it; the pool never shows the customer or full address; a worker is chosen only from the provider's own active workers who can do the service; status changes only through `assertTransition`; the database refuses a pooled job with a provider, an accepted one without, or a worker before assignment; a cancelled plan visit is given back; only the purchaser requests plan visits.
- **Master franchise by city (26-Nov-2026):** `organisations.city_id` (only a master franchise has one; a CHECK and a partial unique index allow one ACTIVE master franchise per city). Its area is every pincode of the city, worked out when asked (`OrgsService.pincodeIdsOf`, `organisationCovering`, `areaOwners`, `masterOf`; a franchise's allowed pincodes read the parent's city), so a pincode added to the city joins at once. Territories of a master franchise are read only (one virtual "<city> (whole city)"); adding one is refused. Changing the city is refused while franchises or providers below hold pincodes, and a switched-off master franchise frees its city. Migration `20261202100000_master_franchise_city` gave existing master franchises the city of their pincodes and removed their old territories.
- **Areas and services (M5.0):** a pincode is held by at most one franchise and a city by one master franchise; a worker can only have services their provider offers (database rule), removing a service from a provider removes it from its workers, only the upline (never the provider) can suspend a service, and a worker's list is private to them, their provider and the upline.
- **Site visit and batches:** nothing but the site visit can be priced for an unverified property (services and plans); a site visit is completed once, only against a paid site-visit order for that property; verified facts cannot be edited by the owner; a batch orders nothing unless every property can be ordered; one payment per batch must equal the unpaid total, and approval pays every order and starts every plan together; a batch is visible only to its purchaser.
- **Onboarding:** a case only moves along allowed steps (an event for each); a franchise or master franchise sees only the cases of its own area and can only read and add notes; an offer is priced by the server and one is open per case (also a database rule); accepting it creates the order at the quoted price; the case becomes active when that order is paid; internal notes never reach the customer; the public form always answers the same way.
- **Maker-checker payments:** the admin who records a payment can never approve or reject it (also a database rule); only approval marks the order PAID (and starts a plan) in one transaction; the amount must equal the order total; a bank reference or a proof slip is used once; one payment waits per order and one is approved per order; an order with a payment waiting cannot be cancelled; the proof is filed in the payer's vault (Payment receipts), locked, and every view of it is logged.
- An order stores its full quote; later price changes never alter it. An order becomes PAID only through the payments step (`markPaid`).
- Every change is audited.

## Known gaps and notes
- Email is sent through the outbox but the sender is still the console printer (no real email until the AWS/SES step).
- Virus scanner is a no-op in development; ClamAV is required before production.
- No real file storage service yet (local disk); S3 at the AWS step.
- Documentation now lives in git: `mpd-handbook-developer/docs/` (this file, the specs, legacy notes, the service seed) and `mpd-handbook-developer/workspace/` (the workspace `CLAUDE.md`, a new-machine guide and setup script).
- GitHub Actions: blocked until the `mpd-infra` repo's Actions access setting allows other repos to use its workflows.
