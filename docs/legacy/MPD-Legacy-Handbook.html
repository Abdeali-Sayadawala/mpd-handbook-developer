The owner journey, from inquiry to active plan

This is the main path for an owner who signs up on the website. Go through it one step at a time.

Two other paths exist. Project owners (builders) are created by the admin, and their payments skip the gateway. Legal and one-off services use dynamic form pages before payment.

A service job, start to finish

Each job is a row in service_purchases. Its screens are built from admin-defined pages (start OTP, before photos, extras, after photos, end OTP, feedback), so different services can have different steps.

Prices are set per pincode

Every price depends on where the property is. That lets each franchise territory charge its own rates.

Site-visit charge

A flat fee per pincode (pincodes.site_visit_charge), paid before MPD inspects the property.

Plan price

Plans A–D and Premium are priced per pincode in a JSON field (pincodes.plans_price). Validity is 6 or 12 months.

Service rate

Service × pincode × area unit (sq ft, acre, guntha, bigha…): 2,799 rate rows, imported from Excel.

What a plan contains

A plan holds services with a recurrence count (plan_services) plus free services. Each one becomes a job when the plan is paid.

Discounts and upsell

Quantity discounts by date range (plan_discounts). Suggested follow-up services after a job. Coupons exist but have never been used.

Payment rails

Razorpay Payment Links in INR. Updates go by WhatsApp through Interakt, currently fixed to +91 numbers.
