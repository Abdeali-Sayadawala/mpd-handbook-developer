Data and outside services

14 modules, each with one job

Each module owns its own tables and rules. Modules talk through services and events, never by reading each other's tables. That's what stops the legacy copy-paste from coming back.

A job can only move one legal step at a time

Statuses are a fixed list, and one class decides who may move a job forward. Every move is logged and triggers the right notifications. The legacy master OTP is replaced by an audited ops override that needs a reason, a photo and a location.

Money is only marked paid when it's proven: offline now, online in M8

    Document vault: every paper about the property, in one place

    Each property gets ready-made folders. Files are private and checked before anyone can open them. Every view is logged.

    Safe uploads

    Type checked from the file contents, 20 MB limit, virus scan before the file can be opened.

    Private links

    Links expire after 5 minutes. Share a deed with a lawyer or bank using a link with an expiry date and view limit.

    Expiry reminders

    Rent agreements, insurance and property-tax dates send reminders at the owner's local time.

    Aadhaar and DPDP Act

    Only the masked Aadhaar is stored. Customers give consent and can request export or deletion.
